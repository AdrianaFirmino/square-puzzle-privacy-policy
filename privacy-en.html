Privacy Policy — Square Puzzle

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Square Puzzle application, developed by Adriana Firmino, handles information related to its users.

1. Personal Information

Square Puzzle does not require users to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as name, address, telephone number, or Brazilian CPF to use the game.

2. Data Stored on the Device

The game may store certain information locally on the user's device, such as game settings, preferences, and records.

This information is used to maintain the user's preferences and the progress associated with the available game features.

3. Advertising

Square Puzzle may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob is an advertising service provided by Google. Depending on the service configuration, the user's location, and available privacy choices, Google may process certain information to provide, measure, and protect advertising.

Google's data processing practices are also subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

4. Third-Party Services

Square Puzzle may use services provided by third parties, such as advertising services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and practices.

5. External Links

The application may provide links to external pages, such as GitHub and the developer's email address.

When accessing these links, the user will be using services outside Square Puzzle, which have their own privacy policies and practices.

6. Children's Privacy

Square Puzzle does not directly request personal information from its users.

The application should not be used to provide personal information to the developer through the game.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information related to the operation of the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be considered completely secure.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when there are changes to the application, the services used, or applicable legal requirements.

The updated version will be made available on this same page together with the corresponding update date.
